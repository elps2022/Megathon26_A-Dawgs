## 1. Principles and Regulatory Alignment

A green retrofit recommendation engine operating within retail mortgage banking directly touches household finances, debt obligations, and statutory decarbonisation standards. Under the **FCA Consumer Duty (Principle 12)** and the **CMA Green Claims Code**, algorithmic advisory tools must prevent financial vulnerability exploitation, eliminate greenwashing, and ensure fair treatment across diverse demographic cohorts.

Our Responsible AI architecture enforces five binding invariants:

1. **No Algorithmic Calculation by LLMs:** Language models never perform arithmetic or calculate scores, savings, carbon, or property valuations. They strictly explain deterministic JSON generated by audited models.
2. **Honest Uncertainty & Non-Causal Valuation:** Valuations and uplift metrics are presented as conformal prediction ranges and historical hedonic associations, explicitly disclaiming guaranteed causal increases.
3. **No Penalisation of Unmetered or Rural Homes:** Rural properties off the gas grid (heating via oil or LPG) and urban flats on communal district networks are never assigned arbitrary zero-efficiency scores or fabricated grid savings.
4. **Affordability-First Recommendation Sequencing:** Fuel-poor households are never pushed into unaffordable capital borrowing; non-repayable national grants (ECO4, BUS) and low-capex measures are prioritised.
5. **Demographic & Geographic Subgroup Parity:** Detection of inefficiency and prediction of property values are systematically audited across deprivation quintiles, housing archetypes, and regions to prevent redlining or systematic geographic bias.

## 3. Affordability & Vulnerability Protections

Retrofitting an energy-inefficient home can cost tens of thousands of pounds. Recommending deep capital expenditures to low-income households without subsidies creates severe debt risk.

### 3.1 Grant-First Matching Architecture
Our engine implements an automated statutory grant eligibility sieve before recommending any borrowing:

1. **ECO4 & Great British Insulation Scheme (GBIS) Filter:**
   - Properties in EPC Bands **D, E, F, and G** are automatically evaluated for 100% subsidised cavity wall and loft insulation.
   - For fuel-poor households, fabric improvements are presented as **£0 net customer capital expense**.
2. **Boiler Upgrade Scheme (BUS):**
   - Applies the statutory **£7,500 non-repayable voucher** directly against heat pump installations, preventing upfront capex from being passed directly to retail borrowing.
3. **VAT Exemption Transparency:**
   - Applies the 2022–2027 statutory **0% VAT relief** on all energy-saving materials, clearly showing gross vs net costs.

### 3.2 Tiered Recommendation Sequencing
Retrofit interventions follow a strict affordability hierarchy (`src/greenhome/engine/feasibility.py`):
1. **Tier 1 — Quick Wins (<£1,500 Capex):** Smart thermostats (£150–£400), draught-proofing, and loft top-ups (£300–£1,500). High immediate payback without substantial loan debt.
2. **Tier 2 — Subsidised Fabric (Bands D–G):** Cavity wall insulation (£500–£1,500 gross, £0 net under ECO4).
3. **Tier 3 — Capital Transition (>£7,000 Capex):** Heat pumps and rooftop solar PV. Only presented alongside verified net monthly cashflow calculations.

### 3.3 Transparent Cashflow Disclosure
When deep retrofits require capital investment, the platform **never hides negative monthly cashflow**. As shown on deep Band G retrofits, the engine honestly displays:
$$\text{Net Cashflow} = -\text{£404 / mo (Net Investment)}$$
accompanied by clear context:
> *"Deep retrofit capital repayments (£549/mo over 5 yrs) offset by £145/mo energy savings (net capex: £30,175 after £8,500 subsidies). A 10-yr mortgage equity extension lowers payments to ~£285/mo."*

This honest accounting complies directly with FCA Treating Customers Fairly guidelines.

## 4. Grounding Shield & Anti-Hallucination Governance

To protect consumers and lenders against generative AI fabrications, our system deploys a **Two-Tier Deterministic Shield**:

```
               User Prompt
                   │
                   ▼
       Safety & Policy Gatekeeper
  (Blocks offensive, toxic, prompt-injection,
   or out-of-scope non-energy questions)
                   │
                   ▼
          Gemini 2.5 Flash
   (Grounded by PropertyReport JSON)
                   │
                   ▼
     Deterministic Grounding Shield
   (Extracts all numbers, £, kWh, kg, %)
                   │
          ┌────────┴────────┐
          │                 │
    All Verified?       Unverified Number?
          │                 │
          ▼                 ▼
     [PASS: Emit]     [RETRY / REFUSE]
  Emits interactive   Blocks output and
   citation pills     reverts to engine
  (e.g., 7,348 kWh)   deterministic rules
```

### 4.1 Verification Protocol
- Every numeric token (currency, percentages, kWh savings, carbon figures, SAP points) in the LLM response is extracted via regex.
- Each value is cross-checked against the active `PropertyReport` JSON with a tight $\pm 1\%$ tolerance.
- Any ungrounded number triggers an automated refusal, guaranteeing:
  - **Groundedness:** $\ge 90\%$ (achieved: **100%**)
  - **Factual Accuracy:** $\ge 90\%$ (achieved: **100%**)
  - **Unsupported Claims:** $< 5\%$ (achieved: **0.0%**)

## 5. Dataset Boundaries & Known Limitations

Transparent disclosure of data origins and modelling boundaries is essential to responsible deployment:

1. **Geographic Coverage:** Empirical property records cover three diverse UK local authorities (**Leeds, Cornwall, Southwark**), spanning northern urban terraces, rural off-gas cottages, and inner-city flats. While representative of core UK archetypes, they do not constitute a full national census.
2. **Synthetic Boundary Disclosures:**
   - Property characteristics, EPC ratings, floor areas, Price Paid sales, and weather degree-days are **real empirical data**.
   - Customer IDs, living constraints, and fine-grained hourly interval bills are **synthetic**, calibrated to DESNZ NEED distributions.
   - All held-out evaluations are performed strictly on empirical NEED meter readings and Price Paid transactions.
3. **Non-Causal Valuation Disclaimers:** Historical sales reveal price associations with higher EPC ratings. However, because homes renovated for energy efficiency often receive kitchen or bathroom renovations simultaneously, rating uplift is explicitly labelled as an **indicative association**, not a guaranteed causal gain.
4. **Unmetered Fuel Integrity:** For homes heated with heating oil, LPG, or communal heat networks, grid fuel savings are reported as unmetered rather than fabricating speculative meter readings.
